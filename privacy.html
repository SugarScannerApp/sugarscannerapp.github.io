Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Sugar Scanner ("Sugar Scanner," "we," "us," or "our") provides an AI-powered tool that lets you photograph or upload ingredient labels to identify hidden sugars, additives, and related nutrition information. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Label images. When you take a photo or upload an image of an ingredient label, that image is submitted to our servers and processed to identify and analyze the ingredients shown. We use the image only to generate your scan results.

Account and subscription information. If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We receive limited billing information from Stripe (such as your email address and subscription status) — we do not receive or store your full card number.

Newsletter signup. If you subscribe to The Hidden Sugar Weekly, we collect your email address through our email service provider, Kit, to send you newsletter content. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

Usage and analytics data. We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use Sugar Scanner — such as pages viewed, general location (derived from IP address), device type, and referral source. This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you.

Referral data. If you use or generate a referral link, we store limited data (such as referral codes and associated account identifiers) needed to track and credit referrals.

Local scan tracking. To provide free scans before you subscribe, we may store a scan counter in your browser (via local storage or cookies) on the device you're using.

How We Use Your Information

  • To analyze ingredient labels and provide scan results
  • To process subscription payments and manage your account
  • To send the newsletter you've opted into, and respond to support requests
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve Sugar Scanner
  • To detect and prevent abuse of our free-scan limits

Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices: Stripe (payment processing), Google Analytics (site analytics), Kit (email newsletter delivery), Supabase (data storage for referrals), and Netlify (hosting). We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention

We retain account, subscription, and referral data for as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with our legal and accounting obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the email below.

Your Choices

  • You can decline camera or photo access; scanning simply won't be available without it
  • You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time via the subscription management portal
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us

Children's Privacy

Sugar Scanner is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Not Medical or Nutritional Advice

Sugar Scanner is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical or nutritional advice. Always consult a qualified health professional regarding your individual dietary needs.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of Sugar Scanner after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
