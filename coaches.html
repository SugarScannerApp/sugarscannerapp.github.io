Common questions

Everything you need to know

How is this different from just sending clients to sugarscannerapp.com?
Your white label version carries your brand — your logo, your colors, your URL. Clients associate the tool with your practice, not a third party. It reinforces your expertise and keeps clients connected to you every time they use it.
Do my clients need to download anything?
No. The scanner works in any mobile browser — iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, or any other. Clients just visit the URL and start scanning. No app store, no download, no friction.
How many clients can use it?
Unlimited. The monthly fee covers unlimited client scans — there are no per-user or per-scan charges. Share it with your entire client base.
What does setup involve from my side?
Very little. You provide your logo, preferred brand colors, and custom URL preference. I handle all the technical setup. You review a preview, approve it, and you're live. No technical skills required.
What ingredients does it identify?
Sugar Scanner identifies over 60 names for sugar and sweeteners including maltodextrin, high fructose corn syrup, brown rice syrup, agave nectar, fruit juice concentrate, maltitol, sucralose, acesulfame K, and many more — each with its glycemic index, health effects, and how it's processed in the body.
